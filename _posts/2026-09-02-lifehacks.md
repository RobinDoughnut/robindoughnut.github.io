# survival and pro tips guide and things i learned on europe trip

Ever since i was a kid i used to be fascinated by random lists on the internet, like how to survive a polar bear in 9gag, reddit threads. So i thought I would curate a list for life hacks, pro tips and things I have learned.

- Bring a portable bodna (bideshis call them bidets) they are a lifesaver.
- Take power bank.
- Carry an empty water bottle and sunscreen.
- Always carry a Visa or Mastercard, two if you can, for emergencies. Cash is not accepted in a lot of places.
- Virgin eSIM is great. The 5GB package will feel like a 30GB package, it lasts a long time.
- You have to pay for bags in most stores. Mother nature will thank you for that.
- Avoid taxis if you can. Use the IDF Mobilités app in Paris for navigation. Buy a single ticket per ride for the train or bus in Paris. In Rome, buy the 24 hour pass.
- The American tourists may seem annoying but they are super friendly once you get to know them. Maybe it's because we understand what they're saying and they describe everything out loud. My experience with them was good. Try to help them if they get lost or something and they will reward you ten fold.
- Northern French people are friendlier and will talk more. They have the community cottagecore vibe going on.
- If you like history, go to Caen and see the castle and the abbey built by William the Conqueror. Aegon the Conqueror in Game of Thrones borrows heavily from him, both launched sea-borne invasions from smaller territories (the Duchy of Normandy for William, Dragonstone for Aegon) to conquer a larger and wealthier mainland realm.
- Take the train to Bayeux for the medieval festival if you're there the first weekend of July. Then take the bus to the war cemeteries. You can do the full package if you're really into WWII, or just pick the ones you want, German, French, American. I like Saving Private Ryan, so I went to the American cemetery, which sits above Omaha Beach.
- When in Paris be rude and walk fast, people will thank you are local and you will be less likely to be scammed.
- Italy is not like the sopranos, they do not say o marone! They will laugh at you if you say that.
- Risotto is overrated.
- Italian food is good because of its fresh ingredients, it is light and not like the explosion of spices we get in Bangladesh
- Most boulangeries in France are good. I think I will write a separate page about food I ate.
- Its ok to embarass yourself in the pursuit of human connection.
- The French loves Dembele and Barcola more than Mbappe.
- Use headphones and walk fast so you can to avoid communication, and appreciate the beauty around you.
- Do not use headphones and talk to people so that you can immerse yourself more and not listen to music and avoid thinking.
- Do not give your shoe to ur Bangladeshi bro. You might not get it back.
- Learn some French and Italian phrases and they will appreciate you.
- Speaking English and French there is easier than in Bangladesh, because they are not obsessed with accents and random pronunciations like *kwasooh*.
- Italians are much more friendly and expressive also they tend to be more touchy.
- If you plan to buy luxury goods, make sure to buy it from one place, like galeries lafayette, so you cross the 100 euro threshold. This will allow you to get the vat refund when you go back home. You will need a international credit card.
- Cigarettes are expensive so it is better to buy them before, and it can lead to an interesting conversation with your professor in a smoking break.
- People consume random brainrot content- like 6 7, tung tung sahur. Try to use that esoteric knowledge for socialisng.
- Do not drink or buy cocaine from random people late at night in Paris.
- Take some mango bar and chewing gum with you if you can give them to people.
- The gelato opposite of vatican is worth the price.
- Avoid deshi and indian restaurants. They are overpriced and will give you acid reflux.
- Latins are really kind and will help you take pictures. Their culture is similar to ours.
- Buy leather products from Florence.
- Bond with people over mutual hatred for Argentine football team.
- Give matador kolom to bideshis.
- Do not follow random lists from internet and do whatever you want to do.
